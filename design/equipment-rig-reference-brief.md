# DRILL RUN NEXT GENERATION — Two core drill models for 3D creation

Date: 2026-11-06. Proposed visual references only. Do **not** include other manufacturers' brands or logos in the game.

## 1 — PIONERO RC · Exploración / sondaje de circulación reversa
Reference: Epiroc **Explorac 235**, official page: https://www.epiroc.com/en-ie/products/drill-rigs/exploration-drill-rigs/reverse-circulation-drill-rigs/explorac-235

- Track-mounted drilling chassis, very tall RC mast/rotation feed, sampling cyclone, pipe rack / robotic pipe handler, outriggers; recognizable independent silhouette.
- Purpose in game: samples and exploration holes, compatibility with EXPLORA® RC downhole tools (product mechanics already modeled in game).
- Reference real-world max depth: up to 450 m (conditions vary), close to game compact RC 400 m. Do not depict an 800 m production mast.
- 3D deliverables: low/medium-poly GLB, PBR-but-stylized material kit matching the approved premium cel-shaded look, no logos except approved game-authorized signs, one idle scene rig with mast raised, one transit/lowered variant optional. Preserve readable pipe/cyclone detail without photoreal noise.
- Dedicated 3/4 front image plus side and rear angles, clean white background. Use original photo only as geometry reference. Model output must not reproduce brand signage.

## 2 — TITÁN DTH · Producción martillo de fondo
Reference: Epiroc **SmartROC D65**, official page: https://www.epiroc.com/es-ec/products/drill-rigs/surface-drill-rigs/smartroc-d65

- Large enclosed cabin, tracked undercarriage, robust engine/compressor housing, tall drilling feed with automatic rod handling and dust collection.
- Purpose: post-exploration production boreholes on a mining bench, using approved PUMA® DTH consumables in the game's tooling system.
- Reference real-world hole diameter 110–229 mm (4.3–9 in), hole depth up to 56 m (Epiroc). Existing game has depth of 800 m, which is not realistic for an individual bench DTH blasthole. This mismatch is identified, NOT silently adjusted in gameplay.
- 3D deliverables: GLB, clearly heavier and wider than PIONERO, mast vertical or tilted, tracked locomotion, dark graphite generic bodywork with appropriate game-safety highlights. Exclude manufacturer logos and any third-party trade dress.
- Ensure distinguishable silhouettes as tiny catalog WebP thumbnails; prepare pre-rendered optimized 2D card image after the 3D model is approved, using the approach established for DrillTruck.

- Equipment model and initial preview to be approved before touching 3D scene assets/colliders; do not model equipment sales as DRILLCO products.
